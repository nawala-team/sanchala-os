## Welcome

Welcome to the SANCHALA OS community! We're building more than just an operating system — we're creating a space where people passionate about security, privacy, and beautiful design can collaborate, learn, and help each other.

These guidelines help ensure our community remains welcoming, productive, and enjoyable for everyone.

---

## Our Values

### 🌟 Respect
Every community member deserves respect, regardless of experience level, background, or perspective. We were all beginners once.

### 🔓 Openness
We believe in transparency — in our code, our decisions, and our communication. Questions and constructive criticism make us better.

### 🤲 Helpfulness
We're here to help each other succeed. A rising tide lifts all boats.

### 🎯 Quality
We value thoughtful contributions over quantity. Take time to craft helpful responses.

### 🔒 Privacy
We respect everyone's privacy and never require personal information to participate.

## Expected Behavior

### Do

✅ **Be welcoming** — Help newcomers feel at home  
✅ **Be patient** — Not everyone has the same experience level  
✅ **Be constructive** — Offer solutions, not just criticism  
✅ **Search first** — Check if your question was already answered  
✅ **Stay on topic** — Keep discussions relevant to the channel/thread  
✅ **Give credit** — Acknowledge others' contributions  
✅ **Report issues** — Help moderators maintain community quality  
✅ **Use clear titles** — Help others find and help with your issue  

### Don't

❌ **No harassment** — Personal attacks, threats, or intimidation  
❌ **No discrimination** — Based on any personal characteristic  
❌ **No spam** — Unsolicited promotion or repetitive content  
❌ **No piracy** — Discussion of illegal software distribution  
❌ **No doxxing** — Sharing others' private information  
❌ **No NSFW content** — Keep it professional and family-friendly  
❌ **No trolling** — Deliberately inflammatory or off-topic posts  
❌ **No impersonation** — Of team members or other users

## Getting Help

### Before Asking

1. **Search** the forum and documentation
2. **Check** the FAQ and known issues
3. **Try** basic troubleshooting steps
4. **Gather** system information (`sanchala-info` command)

### When Asking

**Good question template:**

```
**What I'm trying to do:**
[Describe your goal]

**What I've tried:**
[List steps you've already taken]

**What happened:**
[Describe the actual result, include error messages]

**System info:**
[Output of `sanchala-info` or relevant details]
```

### After Getting Help

- ✅ Mark solutions as accepted
- ✅ Thank those who helped
- ✅ Share what worked for future searchers
- ✅ Consider helping others with similar issues

## Moderation

### Moderation Team

Our volunteer moderators help maintain community standards. They're identified by special roles/badges in each platform.

### Enforcement Levels

| Level | Action | Duration | Reason |
|-------|--------|----------|--------|
| 1 | Warning | — | Minor first offense |
| 2 | Mute | 24 hours | Repeated minor offense |
| 3 | Temporary ban | 7 days | Serious offense |
| 4 | Permanent ban | Indefinite | Severe or repeated serious offense |

### Appeals

If you believe a moderation action was unjust:

1. **Wait 24 hours** before appealing (cooling off)
2. **Email** community@sanchala.id with:
   - Your username
   - The action taken
   - Why you believe it was unjust
3. **Be patient** — Appeals are reviewed within 7 days
4. **Accept the decision** — Final decisions are final

## Security Reporting

### Found a Security Issue?

**DO NOT** post security vulnerabilities publicly.

**DO** email security@sanchala.id with:
- Description of the vulnerability
- Steps to reproduce
- Potential impact
- Your contact information (for follow-up)

We commit to:
- Acknowledging within 48 hours
- Providing updates on fix progress
- Crediting you (unless you prefer anonymity)
- Not pursuing legal action for good-faith research

## Content Guidelines

### Forum Posts

- Use descriptive titles (not "Help!" or "Problem")
- Choose the correct category
- Format code using code blocks
- One issue per thread
- Update thread when resolved

### Screenshots & Logs

- Remove personal information before posting
- Use spoiler tags for long logs
- Compress/upload large files rather than pasting

### Language

- Primary language: English
- Localized subforums available for other languages
- Use translation tools if needed — we appreciate the effort!
