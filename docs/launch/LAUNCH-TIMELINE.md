## Contingency Dates

If launch must be delayed:

| Scenario | New Date | Decision By |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| Minor issues | +3 days | T-3 |
| Moderate issues | +1 week | T-2 |
| Major issues | +2 weeks | T-1 |

Delay criteria:
- Critical security vulnerability discovered
- Infrastructure not ready
- Show-stopping bug in installer
- Legal/licensing issues
