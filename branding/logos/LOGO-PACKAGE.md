## Clear Space Rules

Minimum clear space around logo = Height of "S" in wordmark (or 25% of icon height)

```
        ┌─────────────────────────────────┐
        │         ↑ (min space)          │
        │    ←  [LOGO MARK]  →           │
        │         ↓                       │
        │    ←  SANCHALA  →              │
        │         ↓ (min space)          │
        └─────────────────────────────────┘
```

## Color Variants

| Variant | Usage |
|---------|-------|
| **Full Color** | Light backgrounds, primary usage |
| **White** | Dark backgrounds, overlays |
| **Single Color** | When gradient not possible |
| **Reversed** | Photography backgrounds |

---

## ❌ Logo Misuse

- Do not rotate or skew
- Do not change colors outside brand palette
- Do not add effects (shadows, outlines, 3D)
- Do not stretch or compress
- Do not place on busy backgrounds without contrast
- Do not recreate or modify the icon shape
